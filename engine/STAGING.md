## Options not used yet (pick from these first)
- **Openings:** aerial fly-over from the clouds; a silhouette against a huge moon/sun; an object close-up (weapon, lamp, scroll) that pulls back; a sound-first start (bell, drum) with a fast reveal; a child's-eye view looking up at the god; the end result shown first, then "rewind".
- **Threats:** flood / ocean / storm; a curse shown as shadow or smoke; a forest or mountain creature; an invisible threat (illness, famine, drought); a rival king's court; a trick or disguise (not a fight).
- **Climaxes:** a transformation (small form grows huge); a boon or blessing with light only; a trap closing and breaking; a river or ocean parting; a test of devotion; a silent standoff won by calm; a weapon that is stopped, not thrown.
- **Palettes / places:** forest at dawn; ocean and ships; snowy Himalaya; cave with firelight; royal court in gold; rain and monsoon; desert.
